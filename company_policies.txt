# Acme Corporation Operations & IT Policy 2026

## Section 1: Work Hours and Core Schedules
- Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM EST, Monday through Friday.
- Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM EST, during which all team members must be reachable via Slack.
- Flexible remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

## Section 2: IT Equipment and Reimbursement
- Every new full-time employee is provisioned a MacBook Pro (16-inch, M3 Pro) or a ThinkPad P1 Gen 6.
- The company provides a one-time home office setup stipend of $1,200.
- Monthly broadband internet reimbursement is capped at $75 per month. Submit receipts by the 25th of each month through Expensify.

## Section 3: IT Security and WiFi Credentials
- Corporate Office Network SSID: "Acme-Secure-Corp"
- Guest Network SSID: "Acme-Visitors"
- VPN Gateway URL: vpn.acme-corp.internal:8443
- Passwords must be at least 16 characters long, contain at least one special character, and be rotated every 90 days.
- In case of a suspected security breach, contact the InfoSec Incident Response Team immediately at security-ops@acme-corp.internal or dial extension #4409.

## Section 4: Project Code Names
- Project Falcon: Autonomous analytics pipeline scheduled for Q3 deployment.
- Project Titan: Global multi-cloud data migration effort.
